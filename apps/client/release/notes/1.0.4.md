Forever Dash Client 1.0.4

Brings in [Polestar Client 0.1.7](https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar/releases/tag/client-v0.1.7). The dashboard upload and our settings are unchanged. Installed copies update themselves.

## What's new upstream

- **Tells Polestar about new versions.** After each check for addon updates (and after installing or updating Polestar), Polestar Client writes the newest Polestar release into the addon, so Polestar 0.3.7's update card in the game knows what's out and that the Client can update it for you. The game reads it at your next login or /reload.
