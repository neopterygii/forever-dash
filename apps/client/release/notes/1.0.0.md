Forever Dash Client 1.0.0: Polestar Client with your own dashboard.

- Everything Polestar Client does: finds WoW: Forever, installs and updates Polestar (here from the forever-dash fork's releases, Polestar's own builds with the Polestar_DashSnapshot addon added), community guides, the Recorder.
- **Dashboard** (Settings): sends your recordings, gear, bags and bank to your own Forever Dash server, with an optional device token.
- Sharing with Polestar's servers is **off** unless you turn it on (Settings > Recording > Share my play).
- Uninstall the official Polestar Client first: two clients would both manage the AddOns folder.
- Unsigned: Windows shows a SmartScreen warning ("More info" > "Run anyway").
