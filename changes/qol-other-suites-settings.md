- Quality of Life: with EllesmereUI installed, Polestar's fast loot and
  auto-select of a lone gossip option are no longer off by default:
  EllesmereUI's own Quick Loot and Auto Select Single Gossip start off, so
  until now neither addon did them. A Polestar toggle now starts off only
  while EllesmereUI's setting for the same job is on. Naowh Forever's Quality
  of Life module counts the same way: while its sell junk, repair, fast loot,
  gossip or hide-errors setting is on, Polestar's starts off.
